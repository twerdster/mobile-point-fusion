TUM RGB-D Freiburg 1 xyz — selected recorded RGB/depth and supplied poses

Data authors: Jürgen Sturm, Nikolas Engelhard, Felix Endres, Wolfram Burgard
and Daniel Cremers.

Publication: A Benchmark for the Evaluation of RGB-D SLAM Systems, IROS 2012.

Prepared 2026-11-14 for Aaron Wetzler's updated Mobile Point Fusion adapter.
Changes: selected 120 reconstruction views and 12 disjoint held-out views from
the original sequence; exact pixel-stride-four sampling of depth and RGB from
640x480 to 160x120 without interpolation; nearest RGB timestamps within 30 ms;
interpolated motion-capture poses with both endpoints within 20 ms; conversion to
the first original depth camera's GL basis, x right/y up/-z forward, in metres;
and lossless raw-image-value binary packaging with JSON provenance.

Raw depth values remain unchanged: 5000 units per metre, zero missing.
No additional depth scale correction, undistortion, hole filling or depth
filtering is applied by this converter. Source motion-capture poses are
supplied to fusion; this input does not demonstrate camera pose estimation.
The held-out pack is marked validation_only in its sidecar and must not be
fused into the reconstruction used for its consistency check.

These selected/converted inputs and any reconstructions made from them are
maintenance transformations, not outputs supplied by the dataset authors.

separate from the recovered software's individual source notices.
